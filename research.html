以下列出课题组近期代表性科研成果，主要围绕填埋场抗性组传播、环境表面生物膜演化以及塑料降解酶资源挖掘等方向展开。

  1. 全球填埋场系统抗性组流动特征。Zheng Z, Zhang R, Hong W, et al. Resistome flow in global landfill systems. iMetaOmics, 2025. DOI: 10.1002/imo2.70075
  2. 填埋场源抗生素耐药性特征。Zheng Z, Zhang R, Hong W, et al. Landfills as Hotspots of Multidrug Resistance Genes: Profiles, Drivers, and Hosts. Environmental Science & Technology, 2025. DOI: 10.1021/acs.est.5c13743
  3. 抗生素抗性基因在典型环境表面的富集特征。Zheng Z, Gong Z, Zhang R, et al. Potential pathogens drive ARGs enrichment during biofilms formation on environmental surfaces. ISME Communications, 2025. DOI: 10.1093/ismeco/ycaf057
  4. 填埋场塑料降解酶。Natural-selected plastics biodegradation species and enzymes in landfills. PNAS Nexus, 2025. DOI: 10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf066
